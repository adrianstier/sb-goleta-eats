# Santa Barbara & Goleta Eats

*Last updated: 2026-11-10*

A photo guide, with a street map, to the restaurants, bars, coffee and breakfast spots we recommend around Santa Barbara, the Funk Zone, Montecito and Goleta.

Live site: https://adrianstier.github.io/sb-goleta-eats/

- Places were checked for closures on 2026-11-09 against local news and current listings.
- 43 more places were added on 2026-11-10 from the Eater LA, Michelin Guide, Infatuation, Time Out, Independent Best of 2026 and Visit Santa Barbara lists; each was listed as open on Google Maps that day.
- Hours, Google ratings and open status for every place were read from Google Maps on 2026-11-10. Reservation links come from each place's own website; the patio tag comes from OpenStreetMap and is incomplete.
- Bibi Ji is held back as closed (Siteline, 2026-09-03) even though its Google listing still shows hours.
- 20 more places were added on 2026-11-10: winners of the Independent's Best of Santa Barbara reader vote from 2022 to 2026 that were missing and are listed as open on Google Maps.
- Each place has a recognition score built on 2026-11-10 from Michelin distinctions, critics' guides (Eater LA, The Infatuation, Time Out, Sunset, Tasting Table, Afar, Visit Santa Barbara) and the Independent's 2022 to 2026 reader vote. The 15 highest, plus a few personal favorites, are badged "Our pick" and places are ordered by this score within each type.
- Ten coffee shops and bakeries were added on 2026-11-10 after a survey of Google Maps listings and local coffee guides (Visit Santa Barbara, Downtown Santa Barbara, Edhat, My Coffee Explorer).
- Ten Chinese restaurants were added on 2026-11-10 after a survey of Google Maps listings, the Independent's reader vote and its restaurant coverage. A Chinese collection was added. Meet Up Chinese Cuisine and Pang Zi Noodle Shop are Our picks.
- Thirteen burger places were added on 2026-11-10 after a survey of Google Maps listings, Visit Santa Barbara's best burgers guide, the Independent's reader vote and its Burger Week coverage. A Burgers collection was added and Third Window Brewing became an Our pick.
- Twelve Mexican restaurants and taquerías were added on 2026-11-10 after a survey of Google Maps listings and taco guides (Visit Santa Barbara, Downtown Santa Barbara, L.A. Taco, Alta).
- Pink pins mark new (2024–2026) places getting a lot of local and national coverage.
- Map tiles and data © OpenStreetMap contributors; the map library is Leaflet, loaded from cdnjs.
- Saved places are kept in your own browser and are not sent anywhere.
- One photo (Blenders in the Grass) is by afagen on Flickr, used under CC BY-NC-SA 2.0, cropped and resized.
- Other photos come from each restaurant's own website, its Google Maps listing, or local news coverage (Santa Barbara Independent) and remain the property of their owners. Every photo is credited and linked to its source. If you own one and want it removed, open an issue and it will be taken down.

The page is a static `index.html` plus resized photos in `img/`, with no build step.
